Privacy Policy

AI Assistant Sidebar is designed to keep your data private.

What data is collected?

No personal data, analytics data, or chat history is collected by AI Assistant Sidebar.

How are API keys handled?

API keys you enter are stored locally in your browser extension storage. They are not uploaded to our servers.

Where is key data sent?

Your API key is only transmitted directly to the AI provider you choose (such as OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Claude) to process your requests.
